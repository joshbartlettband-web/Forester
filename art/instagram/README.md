# From @dochwah

Artwork by the designer, from Instagram, for use in Halsey. `sources.json` has each post's date, link and caption.

- **Arches** (`arch-*.jpg`): ceramic sculptures. `cutouts/` holds each one cut out of its photo (Gemini replaced the background with flat green, then it was keyed out) plus an `-opening.png` mask of the space under the arch. In the game they are the Easement's thresholds: one stands in each place, and you walk through it to the next.
- **Paintings:** `painting-door-in-the-hill.jpg` and `painting-same-place.jpg`, used whole as places (era pockets).
- **technical-desert.jpg:** the cracked red-to-blue panel. `card-back-cracks.jpg` is the crop used for card backs.
