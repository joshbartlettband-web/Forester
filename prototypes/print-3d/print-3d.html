What you are seeing

About 2,200 pines and cedars, a fire lookout with a lamp, a campfire on the lane and a pair of eyes in the cedars. The 3D scene is drawn in plain gray. A shader then splits it into ink layers, halftones the in-between tones, knocks ink out where the paper tooth is deep and nudges each screen slightly off register. Choose Raw 3D to see the scene before printing.

What 3D adds

  • The camera can walk the lane, and you can drag to look around.
  • Lamplight actually falls on the nearby trunks.
  • Depth and fog come from real distance.

What it costs

  • Watch the frame counter on your phone, and how warm it gets after a few minutes.
  • Trees close to the camera look simpler than the drawn 2D trees.
  • Because the paper stays still while the world moves, it reads more like animation than a print.
