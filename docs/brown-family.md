## The tellers

- **Metta Brown**, Hummel Hill, Ponca (column 6). She wants Kin. Her reservation: "Won't waste a thing: wants to hear what you kept" (Spare), because the sandbags were dried and sold as sacks. Six stories.
- **Velzoe Brown**, Tech High, Omaha (columns 5 and 6). She wants Leaving. Her reservation: "Wants to hear about a chance you took" (Risk). Six stories.
- Their portraits come from the family's own photographs, which the family cleared: Metta from "Ruth & Metta (7 or 8 yrs.)" in "Ruth's Story" (about 1926), and Velzoe from her 1930 Los Angeles studio portrait on the first bio page. Each is the photo plus a pen drawing made from it, the same way the other tellers are shown. The earlier object drawings (Metta's piano stool with glass-ball claw feet, Velzoe's Holton trombone) are now card art.
- Both carry `family: true`. The teller sheet says "A real person, from the Brown family's own pages. The stories are in her own words," and on a computer a gold "In her own words" tag sits by their wants.
- New places are Ponca (the farm on the hill, with sandbags drying on the fence) and Tech High (the brick school with streetcar rails), each with day, night and sky-mask renders. New eras are 1919 (Ruth's birth) and 1926 (Velzoe's 1920s); same-era cards combine.

## Left out, on purpose

The passages left out are quoted in full, for the family to decide on, in the shared doc "Brown Family Passages Left Out of the Game".

- **The slavery passage in "Ponca School"**, about enslaved people carrying wood to the steamboats. It's secondhand family lore about real suffering, and a card game isn't the place for it without more care.
- **References to Native people**: "deer, bobcats, and Indians," "Indian Acres," and the 1928 "Indian Band" stage show of mostly non-Native women. Native characters and material stay out until there's consultation.
- **Living people named in the draft bio**: friends, students, photographers and the book's own editors. Only Metta and Velzoe, and family members inside their own stories, appear.
- **The bio's own prose and photographs.** Only the Browns' own words are used.

## Balance

With the bot at 2,000 runs: Metta is believed 65% of the time and Velzoe 63%, against 56 to 80% for the others in columns 5 and 6. A Water reservation made Metta too hard (27%) because few decks carry Water; Spare fits her and plays fair.
